# Evidence standards

Public claims should be identified as one of the following:

- **Objective:** a capability or experiment proposed for future consideration.
- **Code exists:** relevant implementation appears in source; this alone does not prove correctness.
- **Historically reported:** a past test or private observation was documented, with date and conditions where available.
- **Currently verified:** a recent, repeatable test supports the claim under stated conditions.
- **Publicly demonstrated:** visitors can reproduce or independently inspect the specific evidence published.

Do not equate a passing test with proof of broad AI capability. Publish test conditions, limitations, software version and date alongside performance claims. Do not claim autonomous operation, accuracy, safety or production readiness without proportionate evidence.

Educational explanations should distinguish documented harms, plausible scenarios and contested future predictions, with citations to primary sources where practical.
